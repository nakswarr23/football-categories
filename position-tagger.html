Position Tagger

Internal tool — not part of the live game. Tags exact sub-positions (LB/CB/RB, CDM/CM/CAM, LW/ST/RW) so the draft can eventually enforce real positions instead of broad groups. Versatile players can get more than one tag (e.g. Konrad Laimer: CM + RB) — every sub-position button is always available regardless of the player's broad DB position.
